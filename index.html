Why it matters

A stock token isn’t always worth its stock.

Markets close. Tokens don’t.

At night and on weekends the stock price stands still, but the token keeps trading on news, hype and fear.

Thin pools. Bigger swings.

A single large trade in a shallow pool can push the price far from fair value. Depth matters as much as price.

One number. Clear picture.

Paritra turns all of it into a single premium or discount per token, with the source and time behind it.

Docs

The short version

Premium and discount

Paritra compares a token’s price with its stock’s price and shows the difference in percent.

(token − stock) ÷ stock × 100

Positive means the token trades above the stock (premium). Negative means below (discount).

Market status

Open: the stock price is live during regular US market hours.

Closed: nights, weekends and holidays. The stock price is the last official close, so the gap shows how far the token has moved since then.

Data sources

Token prices, liquidity and trades come from public pool data on Robinhood Chain. Stock prices come from a market data provider. Every number on the board shows its source and update time.

Limits

Shallow pools can show large, short-lived gaps. Prices can lag by up to a minute. Paritra shows data; it doesn’t tell you what to trade.

FAQ

Good to know

What is a stock token?

A token on Robinhood Chain that follows a real stock, like TSLA or NVDA, and can trade 24/7.

Why do the prices differ?

When the stock market is closed, news still moves the token while the stock waits for the next open. Thin liquidity can widen the gap too.

Is Paritra part of Robinhood?

No. Paritra is an independent project built on public Robinhood Chain data.

Where do I find the real contract address?

Only on this website (top section and the Token section) and on Paritra’s official X and Telegram. Anything else may be fake.

Is this financial advice?

No. Paritra shows data with its sources. Always do your own research.
